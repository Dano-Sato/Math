Finite Screening Conjecture (FSC)

Let (T) be a spherically symmetric rooted tree with bounded integer branching sequence

[
b_0,b_1,b_2,\dots
]

and let

[
b:=\operatorname{br}(T).
]

For a finite consecutive block

[
w=(c_1,\dots,c_m),
]

define

[
K_b(w)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\sqrt b & \sqrt{c_1} & & \
\sqrt{c_1} & 0 & \ddots & \
& \ddots & 0 & \sqrt{c_m}\
& & \sqrt{c_m} & \sqrt b
\end{pmatrix}.
]

Call (w) screened if

[
\lambda_{\max}(K_b(w))\le 2\sqrt b.
]

Let (\mathcal C_b) be the set of screened finite blocks.

The conjecture is

[
\boxed{
\rho_{\mathrm{ess}}(T)=2\sqrt{\operatorname{br}(T)}
\iff
(b_n)_{n\gg1}\in\mathcal C_b^\omega.
}
]

That is:

A bounded-integer spherically symmetric tree has minimal essential spectral radius iff its branching tail can be partitioned into finitely screened blocks.

The nontrivial direction is

[
\rho_{\mathrm{ess}}(T)=2\sqrt b
;\Longrightarrow;
\text{the tail admits an exact screened-block factorization}.
]

Useful test case: for background (b=2), the defect block ((3,1)) is exactly screened.
