## Scalar KPZ Rigidity Conjecture

Let \(T\) be a stationary ergodic finite-range scalar directed random metric on the plane. Assume, near an interior direction \(v\):

1. the deterministic limit shape is \(C^2\) and strictly curved;
2. the microscopic directed geometry is uniformly irreducible, with no persistent hidden sectors/channels;
3. the local disorder is light-tailed, e.g. has a finite exponential moment;
4. passage-time fluctuations have nondegenerate KPZ order:
\[
0<
\liminf_{n\to\infty}n^{-2/3}\operatorname{Var}T(0,nv)
\le
\limsup_{n\to\infty}n^{-2/3}\operatorname{Var}T(0,nv)
<\infty.
\]

### Conjecture
After deterministic centering and affine \(1{:}2{:}3\) KPZ rescaling, the full directed metric locally around \(v\) converges in law to the directed landscape.

Equivalently,
\[
\boxed{
\text{finite-range locality}
+\text{light tails}
+\text{scalar composition}
+\text{irreducibility}
+\text{curvature}
+\chi=\tfrac13
\Longrightarrow
\text{directed landscape}.
}
\]

### Counterexample target
Construct a model satisfying all assumptions above with
\[
\operatorname{Var}T(0,nv)\asymp n^{2/3}
\]
whose rescaled metric does not converge to the directed landscape.

Previous easy attacks fail via long-range dependence, hidden noncommunicating channels, loss of scalar composition, or heavy-tailed critical defects.
