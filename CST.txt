Investigate the **Complementary Spanning Tree Conjecture (CST)**. Independently audit all prior proofs and computations.

**1. Main conjecture**

For every simple graph G on n≥4 vertices with both G and its complement connected,

\[
\tau(G)\tau(\bar G)\ge(n-3)(n-1)^{n-4}.
\]

Equality iff G or its complement is a star with one edge subdivided, \(B_n\).

**2. Established identities**

- \(\tau(B_n)=1\), \(\tau(\bar B_n)=(n-3)(n-1)^{n-4}\).
- Spectral identity: \(\tau(G)\tau(\bar G)=n^{-2}\prod_{i=2}^n\lambda_i(n-\lambda_i)\).
- For a tree T: \(\tau(\bar T)=n^{-1}\det((n-2)I-A(\mathcal L(T)))\).
- For double stars \(D_{a,b}\): \(\tau(\bar D_{a,b})=ab(n-1)^{n-4}\), where \(n=a+b+2\).

**3. Tree-CST — claimed proof, audit required**

For every nonstar tree T,

\[
\tau(\bar T)\ge(n-3)(n-1)^{n-4},
\]

with equality iff \(T=B_n\).

Proposed proofs use leaf relocation, Csikvári/Kelmans tree shifts, and Resolvent Degree Monotonicity (RDM):

\[
\deg(u)>\deg(v)\implies (M^{-1})_{uu}>(M^{-1})_{vv},
\quad M=(m+1)I-L(F).
\]

Audit Schur-complement arguments, spectral bounds, strict inequalities, and the complete relocation chain.

**4. Tree Reduction (TR) — central open problem**

\[
\boxed{\min_{T\subseteq G\text{ spanning tree}}\tau(\bar T)
\le\tau(G)\tau(\bar G).}
\]

TR + Tree-CST implies CST. General TR remains unproved.

For \(H=\bar G\), \(S=E(G)\setminus E(T)\):

\[
\frac{\tau(\bar T)}{\tau(H)}
=\det(I+B_S^\top L_H^+B_S)
\le\prod_{e\in S}(1+R_H(e)).
\]

Investigate global spanning-tree selection, determinantal inequalities, effective resistance, and graphic matroids.

**5. Prior evidence and failed strategies**

- CST/TR reportedly verified exhaustively through n=7; Tree-CST tested through approximately n=12–14.
- Partial TR proofs for unicyclic, cactus, theta, and bicyclic graphs require independent audits.
- Uniform-average TR and greedy single-edge descent reportedly have counterexamples.
- Stronger Hadamard-product sufficient conditions may fail even when TR holds.
- Tree-CST alone does not imply CST.

**6. Research priorities**

1. Certify or refute Tree-CST, RDM, and the claimed Csikvári proof.
2. Prove or disprove TR using exact arithmetic and global determinant methods.
3. Prove or disprove CST, including equality uniqueness.
4. Verify novelty against Kelmans (1976), Kelmans–Chelnokov (1974), Csikvári, and spanning-tree Nordhaus–Gaddum literature.
